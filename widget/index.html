DMZ 2.0 widget

One square tile on your iPhone home screen showing e.g. 24d 14h, same look as the page. No prices. Uses the free Scriptable app.

Get Scriptable (App Store) 2 · Open Scriptable

Then, in Scriptable

  1. Tap + (top right) to make a new script.
  2. Long-press in the empty editor → Paste.
  3. Tap the name at the top ("Untitled Script") and rename it DMZ 2.0. Tap Done.

Then, on the home screen

  1. Long-press an empty spot → + (top left) → search Scriptable.
  2. Pick Small (the square one) → Add Widget.
  3. Tap the new widget once → Script: DMZ 2.0 → done. Tapping the widget later opens the countdown page.
